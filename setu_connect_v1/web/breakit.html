What this does NOT protect you from — stated plainly

  • Someone who has your unlocked phone. Encryption ends where the screen begins.
  • The person you are talking to. They can screenshot anything you send.
  • Perfect forward secrecy: this build uses a fresh key per message, not the Signal double ratchet yet. That is scheduled before public launch, and until then this claim is not made.
  • Voice/video calls travel peer-to-peer, so the two callers see each other's IP addresses. A relay that hides that is planned, not shipped.
  • No independent security audit has been done yet. Everything above is running in front of you — that is evidence, not a certificate.

The cryptography here is standard and battle-tested (Ed25519, X25519, AES-256-GCM) — we did not invent any of it, and you should not trust anyone who invents their own. What is ours is the grammar around it: identities that are signed capsules, connections that require both sides, and messages whose signature binds their content.
