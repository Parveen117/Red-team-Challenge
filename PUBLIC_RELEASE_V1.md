## Finite-model boundary

Every claim is limited to its declared finite protocol.

For CELEXTRIX Impossible Return v2-v4, the admitted object is a closed route over `N,E,S,W` with length 8 through 64 under the published finite-field rules.

This release does **not** claim browser, account, DNS, hosting, cryptographic, physical, or unrestricted continuous-path security.

The public challenge is authorization to test only the published finite challenge artifacts. It is not authorization to attack unrelated services, accounts, infrastructure, devices, or people.
